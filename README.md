> When thinking of Chiikawa, many will picture a timid, shy, scared and anxious little crybaby. While Chiikawa definitely has these qualities, their character is so much more than that.
>
> **Bravery:** Chiikawas scared and anxious nature is actually what makes their character the perfect depiction of bravery, in that despite all the fear, Chiikawa persists and pushes through. That's why Chiikawa is brave, not because they have no fear but because they persist in spite of it. In moments of doubt and anxiety, I find myself thinking about Chiikawa and being motivated to push the fear aside and do it.
>
> **Heroism:** While most might think of Chiikawa as the weakest and most endangered of the cuties in the show, I would argue the opposite. Chiikawa might be the most scared and crying one of all of them, but in my opinion they are also the most heroic of them. There are many examples of this in the show, where Chiikawa is terrified out of their mind but somehow keeps fighting and ends up on the top of the situation. Chiikawa is ALWAYS there for others when they're in trouble or danger and will do anything for their friends. Examples:
>
> 1. When Chiikawa stepped in after 3 of the pyjama parties members were taken by the chimera, despite having a big case of stagefright Chiikawa stepped in for a complete stranger
> 2. When Usagi was stuck in the big cake screaming for help, Chiikawa almost burst at the seams eating Usagi out of it
> 3. When all 3 of them were sleeping at Hachiwares place and the creepy bug came, Chiikaw didn't want to disturb their friends so they took them on all by themself
> 4. **(SPOILERS FOR THE MOST RECENT ANIME ARC)** When both Usagi and Hachiware were on deaths doorstep, Chiikawa masterfully took out the evil snake statue and saved their friends from certain doom
>
> In conclusion, Chiikawa is a simplistic looking show that delves much deeper than most would think. I am a 26 year old grown a*s man and I can't count how many times this show has moved me emotionally and taught me things I've never even thought about. It has made me laugh, cry and think more than most other shows ever have.
